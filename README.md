# hyFen8ed GPS: The Customizable Life Visualizer

A sovereign, 100% private, customizable life operating dashboard and visualizer.

## Core Features
- **Inbound Capture & Ingestion**: Share any Facebook Reel, YouTube Short, or article from Chrome or mobile share sheet.
- **Multimodal AI Analysis**: Scans spoken audio, video screenshots (OCR/diagrams), and captions to extract actionable knowledge.
- **3 Master Pillars**: Personal Life, Health & Care, and Financial Hub.
- **Universal Daily Journal**: Timestamped notes (`MM-DD-YYYY hh:mm AM/PM`) routed to life hubs with full memoir lookback.
- **The True Anchor**: Direct link to [Bible Compass](https://biblecompass.hyfen8ed.com).
